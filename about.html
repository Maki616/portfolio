About Me

Hi! I'm Gian Jalandoni. Growing up, I always loved art and drawing, but over time, I became interested in systems and programs and eventually pursued the field of Information Technology. Even though I entered IT, my passion for creativity stayed with me and now inspires the way I design and develop projects.

I enjoy designing clean and creative user interfaces for websites and applications. I want to continue developing projects that will allow me to improve both my creativity, problem-solving and coding abilities. I want to experiment with different layouts, styles, and interactive features to make fun projects. Learning new concepts and methods is what motivates me to continue improving every day.
